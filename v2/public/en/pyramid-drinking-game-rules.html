The rules of the Pyramid drinking game

Pyramid is a card drinking game for 2 to 12 players with a standard 52-card deck. It rests on one simple idea: you know your cards, the others don't — and nothing forces you to tell the truth.

Play it without cards

La Gitanerie is a free web app that runs Pyramid across phones: everyone sees their own cards on their own screen, nobody can peek, and there is nothing to install. Party mode works even with no internet connection.

Start a game

What you need

  • 2 to 12 players. It really comes alive from 4 upwards.
  • A 52-card deck — or the app, which deals for you.
  • Something to drink. Every rule works with whatever is in the glass.

Setting up

  • Each player gets 4 face-down cards. They look at them and memorise them: they will not see them again.
  • The rest of the deck builds a face-down pyramid: one card at the top, two below, and so on down to the base. Five rows is the usual format.
  • Pyramid cards are turned over one at a time, starting from the base and working up to the top.

The heart of the game

  • When a card is revealed, any player holding the same value may say so and hand out sips to whoever they like.
  • The number of sips depends on the height of the row: 1 sip at the base, then 2, 3, 4 going up.
  • The single card at the very top is bottoms up.
  • But nothing checks that you actually hold the card. You can bluff.

Calling a liar

  • Anyone can call “Liar!” on the player who just handed out sips.
  • If they were bluffing, they drink the penalty.
  • If they really held the card, the accuser drinks.
  • This is where the game is won: accusing too often is expensive, never accusing lets the bluffers run free.

The memory round

  • Once the pyramid is exhausted, everyone must recall their 4 cards from memory.
  • 3 sips per mistake. That is what makes the memorising at the start genuinely matter — and what most versions leave out.

Common variants

  • Multiplier: every sip from the pyramid counts double or triple.
  • Public proof: when an accused player proves themselves, everyone sees the card.
  • Shorter or taller pyramid: from 2 to 8 rows depending on how long you want it to last.
  • Spoken mode: the phone only shows your cards, everything else is settled out loud.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an app to play the Pyramid drinking game?

Yes. La Gitanerie is a free web app dedicated to Pyramid: each player sees their four cards on their own phone, and the app handles dealing, sips and the memory round. It needs no install and no sign-up, and its Party mode works with no connection at all.

How many players do you need?

2 to 12. From 4 upwards bluffing becomes genuinely interesting, because enough cards are in play for a claim to be believable.

How many sips per card?

One sip for the bottom row, then one more for each row going up. The card at the very top is bottoms up.

Can you play without alcohol?

Yes, with no changes. Sips work with any drink: the game is about reading people, not about what is in the glass.

How is it different from Kings Cup?

Kings Cup ties a rule to each card value. Pyramid is built on hidden cards and bluffing: it is a game of reading the other players, not a list of forfeits.

This game features alcohol consumption and is intended for adults. Every rule works just as well without alcohol.
